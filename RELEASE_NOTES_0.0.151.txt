Obsidian Bags 0.0.151 - EXPERIMENTAL COMBAT BACKGROUND RECEIVER

This is an opt-in diagnostics build. Existing protected combat presentation,
normal inventory, bank transactions and physical sorting remain unchanged.

WHAT TO TEST
  Out of combat: /obplace arm, then /obplace background on.
  Arrange one small Linen Cloth stack in physical Bag 0 and leave Bag 1
  equipped with one empty slot and NO Linen Cloth at all.
  In combat, split 2 Linen onto the actual item cursor, then LEFT CLICK
  genuine blank content of the EXPANDED inventory (not the native slots).
  Inspect both physical bags. Expect bag0 remainder -2, bag1 new stack +2.
  Report BLANK ATTEMPT and BLANK RESULT lines. Optionally try a real drag
  over blank inventory and see whether BLANK ATTEMPT event=drag fires.
  After combat: /obplace background off and /obplace disarm.

SAFETY
  On blank input IN combat only, addon checks for live Linen itemID 2589
  on cursor and scans physical Bags 0 and 1. It will choose the first
  completely readable bag having 1+ empty physical slots and NO existing
  Linen Cloth. If none fits, the cursor remains untouched. A successful
  native call will physically move or merge linen according to Blizzard.
  It is deliberately *not* a universal first-empty-slot implementation.
  The native item buttons sit above the experimental blank canvas receiver.
  No fallback, retries, ClearCursor, protected PickupContainerItem calls,
  synthetic clicks or item-cache writes.

Note: v0.0.149 combat-exit category visibility fix remains included.
