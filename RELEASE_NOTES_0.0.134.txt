FOCUSED FIXES
- Bank One Stack remains enabled after left-clicking a physical stack in its
  inspector. The old "reveal" action silently set bankOneStack=false and
  left settings visually out of sync. The new left-click only selects and
  highlights the verified physical source within the inspector; it does not
  reflow the bank or change any persisted preference.
- Right-click continues guarded quick-withdraw. Shift-left continues
  guarded physical stack splitting. No change to their physical engines.
- Inspector labels show "Stack 1   x20" etc., with no bank bag/slot indices.
- Removed the physical bank slot coordinate from ordinary bank item tooltip.
- Bank Standard/Category, collapse-to-unique, summary totals, tabs, frame
  sizing, linked settings and all secure combat architecture unchanged.
