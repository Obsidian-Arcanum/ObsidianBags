Obsidian Bags 0.0.162 — utility empty-slot presentation

Normal out-of-combat Category View only:
- Genuine empty slots from the dedicated utility/specialty sections (equipped quiver/ammunition/Soul Bag, and dedicated reagent bag) are shown inside UTILITY, rather than in general AVAILABLE SLOTS.
- Collapsed UTILITY shows its existing occupied One Stack summaries but no empty tiles. An empty-only collapsed section retains its heading/expansion arrow.
- Expanded UTILITY presents those real native physical empty slots, including in One Stack mode.
- If UTILITY is hidden by a user rule, mandatory MISCELLANEOUS receives the physical utility empty slots rather than losing a destination.
- No changes to utility contents' classification, custom category rules, Standard View, bank, SELL, physical sorting, SavedVariables, protected combat code, or combat-expanded preparation.

Client test: with an equipped quiver and at least one normal free slot, verify Available Slots counts only ordinary free cells; UTILITY expanded shows the quiver's own real blank cells; collapse hides only its blanks, expand restores. Recheck One Stack and combat B/expanded slots as a smoke test.
