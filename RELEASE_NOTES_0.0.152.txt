Obsidian Bags v0.0.152 | EXPERIMENTAL DIRECT EMPTY SLOT COMBAT DROP

One opt-in experiment beyond v151: on an explicitly enabled combat blank
background click/drop, choose the FIRST VERIFIED EMPTY PHYSICAL SLOT across
Bag 0 and equipped Bag 1, and attempt one direct Blizzard
C_Container.PickupContainerItem(bagID,slotID) call. This API may be blocked
by the client's protected-action checks in combat; Lua pcall success alone
is NOT proof that a move occurred.

PREP: Out of combat, equip Bag 1 and keep at least one physically EMPTY slot
in Bag 0 or Bag 1. Keep a small Linen Cloth stack (item 2589) IN Bag 0 or 1,
with NO requirement to move other Linen out of the target bag. Open Obsidian
Bags, and type /obplace arm followed by /obplace direct on (BOTH out of combat).
Enter combat, split 2 Linen Cloth from a physical stack (leave a visible
remainder). While held, LEFT CLICK a genuine blank portion of the expanded
inventory canvas, not an actual physical slot or the separate probe panel.
Observe DIRECT ATTEMPT/RESULT or DIRECT STOP and inspect target bag:slot
from chat using real physical bags. After combat: /obplace direct off and
/obplace disarm.

Stop after ONE attempt if a protected-action warning appears. Do not
repeatedly trigger a blocked API. We need the full DIRECT messages, physical
result, and any protected-action warnings. Cursor is NEVER forcibly cleared.

This is NOT a completed feature and NOT a broadly applicable secure drop
mechanism. Only Linen Cloth 2589 and physical Bags 0/1 are supported, only
with a visible Linen remainder proving a split-like scenario. The addon
rechecks target emptiness immediately before dispatch and refuses uncertain
slots; no auto-merge fallback, retry, cursor-clearing, stored reservations,
or changes to the secure combat native buttons, physical sorting, banking,
or normal inventory. Previous /obplace background on still does v151 auto
placement; DO NOT use that command for this direct experiment.
